## What does it cost to buy Ethereum?

There is no single universal “OKX Ethereum fee.” The total cost may include several components:

### Trading fee

If you buy ETH through the spot market, the fee depends on whether the order is filled as maker or taker and on your account tier. A market order that fills immediately is generally treated as a taker order. A limit order may be filled as a maker or taker depending on how it executes.

### Payment-provider fee

A card, wallet, or third-party payment provider may add its own processing cost. The amount can vary by region and payment method. Review the final checkout screen instead of assuming that a platform statement such as “zero platform fees” means every payment method is free.

### Spread or quote difference

Instant purchases may use a quoted rate that differs from the mid-market price. The practical comparison is the amount of ETH received after all displayed charges.

### Withdrawal fee

Buying ETH on OKX and withdrawing ETH to another wallet are separate actions. Crypto deposits into OKX do not require a deposit network fee, but withdrawals are charged to the sender. OKX says withdrawal fees can vary by network and can change with network conditions.

Before withdrawing, select the correct network and compare the displayed fee. The receiving wallet or exchange must support the same network. Sending ETH through an incompatible network can result in lost funds or a difficult recovery process.

## Common mistakes when buying Ethereum on OKX

### Confusing ETH with an Ethereum-based token

ETH is the native asset of Ethereum. A token built on Ethereum may use a different contract address and may not be interchangeable with ETH for every purpose.

### Ignoring the final quote

The price shown on a chart is not necessarily the exact execution price for an instant purchase. Always compare the order preview with the amount of ETH you will actually receive.

### Assuming every payment method is available

Payment methods vary by jurisdiction and account status. If ACH, Apple Pay, or card payment does not appear, it may not be supported for that specific account.

### Sending ETH over the wrong network

The network selected during withdrawal must match the receiving platform. Do not rely on the asset name alone.

### Treating a referral rebate as guaranteed

The CASH20 invitation link may display a promotional offer, but eligibility rules can depend on region, account status, product, minimum activity, time limits, and campaign terms. Review the conditions shown during registration.

### Using leverage for a simple ETH purchase

Spot buying and leveraged trading are different activities. If your goal is simply to own ETH, use the spot or Buy Crypto flow and avoid derivatives unless you fully understand liquidation, funding, and margin requirements.
